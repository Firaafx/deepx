Here is the best way to experience Star Wars for the first time as of April 2026.

🍁 Phase 1: The Foundation (The Original Trilogy)
Start where the world started. These films establish the lore, the Force, and the iconic characters.

1. Episode IV: A New Hope (1977)
2. Episode V: The Empire Strikes Back (1980)
3. Episode VI: Return of the Jedi (1983)

🍁 Phase 2: The Origins (The Prequel Trilogy)
Now that you know how the story ends, go back to see how it began. These focus on the fall of the Republic and the rise of Darth Vader.

4.  Episode I: The Phantom Menace (1999)
5.  Episode II: Attack of the Clones (2002)
6.  Episode III: Revenge of the Sith (2005)

🍁 Phase 3: Filling the Gaps(The "A Star Wars Story" Films)
These are standalone movies that take place between the Prequels and the Originals. They are best watched here because they add weight to the Original Trilogy.

7.  Rogue One (2016) – Leads directly into the start of A New Hope.
8.  Solo (2018) – The backstory of Han Solo.

🍁 Phase 4: The Conclusion (The Sequel Trilogy)
The final chapter of the "Skywalker Saga," set 30 years after the Original Trilogy.

9.  Episode VII: The Force Awakens (2015)
10. Episode VIII: The Last Jedi (2017)
11. Episode IX: The Rise of Skywalker (2019)

🍁 Phase 5: The "Mando-Verse" (Live-Action Series)
If you still want more, these series are currently the most popular part of the franchise. They take place between Return of the Jedi and The Force Awakens.

The Mandalorian (Seasons 1-3)
The Book of Boba FettAhsoka (Season 1)
Skeleton Crew (Season 1)
Upcoming Movie: The Mandalorian and Grogu (Releasing May 22, 2026).

🍁 Phase 6: The Deep Dives (Other Series)
Once you are a fan, you can tackle these in any order, though Andor is widely considered the best written of the bunch.

Andor: A gritty spy thriller set before Rogue One.
Obi-Wan Kenobi: Follows Obi-Wan during his exile on Tatooine.
The Acolyte: Set 100 years before the movies (The "High Republic" era).
The Clone Wars (Animated): Essential for deep lore; watch the 2008 movie first, then the 7 seasons.
Maul: Shadow Lord: (Premieres in two days, April 6, 2026) – A new animated series following Maul's criminal empire.
